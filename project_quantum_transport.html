Quantum Physics C++ Python Statistical Analysis Numerical Simulations

Project Overview

This project encompasses my PhD research focused on numerical simulations and statistical analysis of quantum transport phenomena in mesoscopic systems, with particular emphasis on the Altland-Zirnbauer symmetry classification.

The research investigated fundamental quantum properties including entanglement, conductance fluctuations, and thermodynamic uncertainty relations in nanoscale devices using advanced computational methods and random matrix theory.

PhD Thesis
"Unveiling the universal class categorization of Altland-Zirnbauer in mesoscopic systems: probing entanglement and conductance fluctuations"
Institution: UFPB, Brazil
Period: 2019 - 2023
Research Focus

Altland-Zirnbauer Classification

Comprehensive framework for categorizing random matrix ensembles based on fundamental symmetries.

Master's Thesis
"Chaos-mediated entanglement in an open quantum dot via the Keldysh formalism"
Institution: UFPB, Brazil
Period: 2017 - 2019

This work investigated how classical chaos in the underlying dynamics of quantum dots influences quantum entanglement generation. Using the Keldysh non-equilibrium Green's function formalism, the research established connections between chaos, decoherence, and quantum correlations.

Key Scientific Contributions

🏆 Universal Behavior

Demonstrated universal statistical properties across different AZ symmetry classes in mesoscopic transport.

🔗 Entanglement & Chaos

Established quantitative relationships between classical chaos and quantum entanglement generation.

⚖️ Thermodynamic Bounds

Derived and verified thermodynamic uncertainty relations in quantum transport scenarios.

💻 Computational Framework

Developed efficient numerical methods for large-scale quantum transport simulations.
